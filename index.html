FAQ & usage terms

Can I use an asset commercially?

For commercial-use permission, email admin@alphaff.gg with the product name and how you plan to use it.

Do I need to give credit?

Email admin@alphaff.gg with the product name to confirm credit requirements for your project.

Can I share or redistribute the files?

Ask admin@alphaff.gg about permission before sharing, reselling, or redistributing asset files.

How do I get help with a purchase?

Email admin@alphaff.gg with the product name, order reference, Blender version, and a description or screenshot of the issue.

Can I request custom work?

Yes. Use the project form on the Contact page to share your brief, budget, preferred deadline, and references. I’ll reply to discuss availability and a quote.
